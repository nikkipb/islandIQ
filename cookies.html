Cookies

Last updated 21 October 2026

IslandIQ itself uses no cookies. It stores a few items in your browser's local storage so the game works, and Google sets cookies for advertising once ads are switched on.

Stored by IslandIQ (essential)

These never leave your device except the player ID, which is sent with each guess. They are needed for features you ask for, so they do not require consent.

NameWhat it doesHow long
islandiq-v2Your progress and scores for each issueUntil you clear browser data
islandiq-playerRandom player ID that keeps your first guess finalUntil you clear browser data or delete your data
islandiq-themeRemembers light or dark modeUntil you clear browser data

Set by Google (advertising)

Google AdSense uses cookies such as __gads, __gpi, IDE and FCCDCF (which stores your consent choice). In the EU these are only used for personalised ads if you agree in Google's consent message. Google lists them in its advertising cookies guide.

Change your choice at any time: . You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the game will still work.

Fonts and hosting

Our fonts are served from our own site, so no font request goes to Google. Cloudflare, our host, may set a strictly necessary security cookie (such as __cf_bm) to tell people from bots.
