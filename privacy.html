Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

IslandIQ is built to collect as little as possible. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number to play. This policy explains what is processed, why, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Malta's Data Protection Act (Cap. 586).

Who is responsible

The data controller is IslandIQ, operated by its founders in Malta. Contact us about privacy at hello@islandiq.gg.

Advertising

IslandIQ shows ads through Google AdSense. Before any ad loads for visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks whether you agree to personalised ads. If you say no, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use cookies only for things like frequency capping and fraud prevention. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects; read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can change your choice at any time with .

Who we share data with

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the site and the database as our processor.
  • Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC provides advertising.

Both may process data outside the EU, including in the United States. Those transfers are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data, and we do not use any analytics or tracking tools beyond what is listed here.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

  • Delete your game data yourself: open past issues (the calendar icon) and choose Delete my data. This erases every guess linked to your player ID from our database and clears the game from your browser.
  • Anything else: email us. Include your player ID, shown in the same panel, so we can find your data.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to Malta's Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC) at idpc.org.mt.

Children

IslandIQ is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly process their data. In Malta, children under 13 need a parent's consent for online services that rely on consent, such as personalised ads.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be shown in the game.
