About

The daily Maltese numbers game

Every day IslandIQ asks three questions about Malta that have a number for an answer: how many tourists came last year, how much waste we recycle, how many people live in a locality. You get one guess each. No looking it up.

Scoring

Your score is how many times off you were. Guess the exact answer and you score 1.00×. Guess half or double the answer and you score 2.00×. Lower is better, and your score for the day is the average of your three.

Where the numbers come from

Every answer comes from an official or primary source, mostly the National Statistics Office (NSO), government departments, Heritage Malta, the Malta FA and UNESCO. Each question is tied to a reference period, because official figures are sometimes revised later. If you think an answer is wrong, tell us and we'll check it.

Who we are

IslandIQ is made in Malta by two friends who like numbers more than is healthy. It is free to play and paid for by advertising.
